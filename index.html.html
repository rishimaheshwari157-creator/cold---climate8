Official Bias Audit Note & Mitigation Strategy (University Project Defense)

1. The Bias Problem Detected in Early Builds: Initial testing revealed a common algorithmic trap where high-GDP Western countries (such as the United States and Germany) and Wholly Owned Subsidiary / FDI entry modes were disproportionately recommended regardless of the input product. For example, tropical agricultural pumps and low-cost EV scooters were incorrectly directed toward developed cold/temperate economies due to unweighted GDP dominance.

2. Root Causes Identified: (a) Static, uncalibrated macro weights that over-indexed on nominal GDP per capita; (b) Lack of negative correlation penalties for climate mismatch; (c) Entry mode scoring that assumed infinite capital without checking startup/SME resource constraints.

3. How We Fixed It (Algorithmic Adjustments): (i) Dynamic NLP Feature Tagging: We implemented a free-text parser that maps typed products to specific demand attributes (e.g., Tropical Solar Pumps activate high sunshine/agricultural demand weights, while Winter Apparel activates sub-zero cold indices); (ii) Relative Market Fit Normalization: GDP is now balanced with sector-specific TAM and bilateral tariff agreements; (iii) Resource-Based View (RBV) Constraint Mapping: Startups and SMEs receive severe penalties for capital-heavy Wholly Owned Subsidiaries, dynamically unlocking Digital D2C, Licensing, or Agent Exporting.

4. Test Verification: The 8 empirical test cases below confirm zero geographic monopoly: winning markets span North America (Canada), Southeast Asia (Vietnam, Indonesia), Middle East (UAE), Africa (Kenya, South Africa), and Latin America (Brazil).
